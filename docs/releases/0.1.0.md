## What is new

The first version of VCSIDE, a small native IDE for making Atari 2600 games on macOS, Windows and Linux.

- **Three kinds of project**: assembly with DASM, batari Basic 1.9, and C for ELF cartridges (PlusCart, UnoCart, Chameleon Cart). New projects start from a template or an example, and folders that build with a Makefile of their own work too.
- **Build, run and debug** with F7, F5 and F6 in Stella or Gopher2600. Errors go to *Problems*, breakpoints go to the emulator on the right address and bank, and *Go to address* takes an address the emulator shows back to its source line.
- **An editor that knows the 2600**: the cycles of every instruction (click them for the line's address), 6502 page separators in assembly, the total of the selected lines in scanlines, help and completion for instructions, registers and your own symbols, folding, and Ctrl/Cmd+click on symbols and included files.
- **Documentation** for all 256 6502 opcodes, the TIA and RIOT registers, cartridge formats and TV standards, with its sources.
- **Space report** after every build: bytes per bank, RAM and stack, cartridge RAM, and for ELF the flash and RAM of the Chameleon Cart or the RAM of the PlusCart and UnoCart, as chosen for the project.
- **Graphics editor** for sprites built from the TIA's objects, flicker, animations and playfields, drawn as the kernel will draw them, with tables for DASM, batari Basic or C kept up to date on save.
- **Sessions**: closing VCSIDE keeps its windows, projects, open files, cursors and breakpoints, and opening it again brings them back. The project panel shows every file and dims what Git ignores.
- **Themes** (Dark, Light, Solarized and your own) and the interface in English and Spanish.
